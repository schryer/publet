## Definition

the concept, defined

> namespace capture: a name in a shared registry coming under the control
> of a party other than the one whose work it names, so that what the
> name resolves to is decided by registration order rather than by the
> reader

## Documented Case

Birsan 2021, dependency confusion

> In February 2021 security researcher Alex Birsan showed that packages
> he published under internal company names to npm, PyPI, and RubyGems
> were installed automatically by the build systems of Apple, Microsoft,
> PayPal, Shopify, and over thirty other companies, because the public
> registry resolved those names before the companies' own private
> packages did.
