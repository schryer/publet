## Definition

the concept, defined

> translation desynchronization: a rendering of a work still presented as
> current after the work it renders has changed, with nothing marking it
> stale

*Asserted, definitional* — `pub:sha2-256:7oxmte5jd6hjkecughukoulo5op7vd4rh2gmf7usv3n5hl7c35wq`

## Documented Case

cross-lingual factual inconsistency across Wikipedia language editions

> Multilingual Wikipedia articles on the same subject are written and
> updated independently rather than as renderings of one source,
> producing factual inconsistencies across language editions that
> automated alignment studies such as InfoGap and Ziggurat were built
> specifically to detect.

*Asserted, empirical* — `pub:sha2-256:glmmoyxtufg5kfl4npy6b6fyjonjxokaxmkx5btg64yjin62fxwq`
