## Definition

the concept, defined

> split-brain forking: one lineage continuing as two divergent heads with
> nothing on either recording that the other exists, so a reader
> consulting one has no way to learn the other was ever said

## Documented Case

### the 2002 Spanish Wikipedia fork: its founding

> On 26 February 2002 a group of Spanish Wikipedia editors led by Edgar
> Enyedy founded Enciclopedia Libre Universal from the existing Spanish
> Wikipedia article base, in protest of a proposed advertising policy.

### its two-decade divergent outcome

> Enciclopedia Libre Universal and Spanish Wikipedia were maintained
> independently for more than two decades with no mechanism linking
> corresponding articles between them, until Enciclopedia Libre closed in
> 2024.
