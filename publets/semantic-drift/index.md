## Definition

the concept, defined

> semantic drift: a term's sense changing over time with no change to the
> term itself, so that a reader recovers a meaning its author did not
> intend

## Documented Case

the scientific/colloquial equivocation on "theory" in public debate over evolution

> Public debate over teaching evolution has repeatedly relied on the word
> "theory" carrying its colloquial sense of an unproven guess where a
> reader encountering it in a scientific context would take the
> established sense of an explanation strongly supported by multiple
> independent lines of evidence.
